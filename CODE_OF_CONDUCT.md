# Code of Conduct

Rolodex is a small open source project, and it should be a place where
people can ask questions, report problems, and contribute without being
made to feel unwelcome.

## Our standards

Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be respectful of other people, their time, and their experience.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on the work rather than
  the person.
- Assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language, and avoid personal attacks,
  insults, and demeaning jokes.

Behavior that is not welcome includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, slurs, or comments that demean someone for who they are,
  including their race, gender, sexuality, disability, religion, age,
  or national origin.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or repeated unwanted contact
  after being asked to stop.

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any
space the project runs for its community.

## Enforcement

If you see behavior that breaks this code, or if someone's behavior is
making it hard for you to take part, report it to gallinij@gmail.com.
Reports are kept private.

The maintainer will review the report, talk to the people involved where
that is appropriate, and decide what to do. Consequences can include a
warning, a temporary removal from the project spaces, or a permanent ban.
The maintainer's decision on a conduct report is final for that report.

If you disagree with a moderation decision, you can reply to the
maintainer once with your view. That reply will be read, even if the
decision does not change.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is written for Rolodex. It follows the spirit of the
Contributor Covenant, but it is the project's own short version, so it can
be read in a minute and enforced by one maintainer.
